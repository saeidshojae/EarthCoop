# EarthCoop Persian SEO Growth Design

Status: design decisions approved in chat; written spec awaiting explicit user review before implementation planning.

Date: 2026-10-05

Base commit: `9f916d80be35925ec6f51b2fac0670cee9b64438`

## 1. Context

EarthCoop already has a technical SEO foundation on `https://earthcoop.ir`: a canonical origin, canonical redirects from `.net`, shared public metadata, Open Graph/Twitter output, structured data, a dynamic sitemap, and an indexable public blog. The next phase is not a small metadata patch. It is a Persian-first semantic/content SEO architecture intended to make EarthCoop discoverable for the concepts it actually represents.

The current site can already be found for the brand `EarthCoop`, but broad Persian searches around cooperation, governance, economic participation, democratic decision-making, alternative monetary systems, justice, shared rights, and related concepts do not yet reliably surface EarthCoop.

This design establishes the strategy for building topic authority without keyword stuffing, thin doorway pages, or semantic claims that exceed EarthCoop's actual documents and implementation.

## 2. Strategic position

EarthCoop is not to be positioned merely as a generic cooperative platform. Its public content should explain that EarthCoop is a global cooperative system designed to realize an explicitly defined model of justice through participatory governance and the economic model called **«اقتصاد آزاد مردمی»**.

The economic model is attributed to **EarthCoop as an entity**, not to a founder personally.

Public descriptions should use formulations such as:

- «الگوی اقتصادی EarthCoop»
- «مدل اقتصاد آزاد مردمی در EarthCoop»
- «چارچوب اقتصادی پیشنهادی EarthCoop»

They should not claim that «اقتصاد آزاد مردمی» is already a recognized academic school or established external doctrine unless independent evidence later supports that claim.

## 3. Audience and language policy

### Phase 1

Primary audience: **all Persian-speaking people worldwide**, not users in one particular country.

Content should therefore:

- use clear standard Persian;
- avoid unnecessary dependence on one national legal or political framework;
- explain concepts in globally understandable language;
- cite country-specific examples only when they genuinely help the topic.

### Later phases

English and Arabic SEO will be added later.

The architecture must be multilingual-ready from the beginning, but keyword research must be performed independently for each language. English and Arabic pages must not be simple literal translations of Persian keyword targets.

## 4. Foundational philosophical layer

Justice, rights, natural/common rights, freedom, equality, human dignity, responsibility, and the proper status of land and shared resources are not secondary SEO decorations. They are foundational concepts behind EarthCoop and must be represented in the topic architecture.

EarthCoop's internal framing of justice is based on putting each thing in its proper place and giving each right to its rightful holder. Within that framework, land and foundational natural resources are treated as matters of common right; common ownership/right grounds participation, oversight, benefit, and responsibility; private ownership of one's legitimate labor and creation remains respected within those boundaries.

The SEO program should therefore include a philosophical-rights cluster covering concepts such as:

- عدالت اجتماعی
- عدالت توزیعی, where semantically applicable
- حق و حقوق
- حقوق بنیادین

- کرامت انسانی
- آزادی
- آزادی مسئولانه
- آزادی عادلانه as an EarthCoop explanatory formulation where appropriate
- برابری
- برابری بنیادین
- برابری عادلانه as an EarthCoop explanatory formulation where appropriate
- حق مشارکت
- حق نظارت
- حق بهره‌مندی
- مسئولیت مشترک

- منابع مشترک / commons

### SEO caution

Very broad words such as «عدالت», «آزادی», «برابری» and «حق» are highly ambiguous and competitive. EarthCoop should not create generic pages that merely repeat these words. They should function as a **foundational semantic cluster**, with targeted pages and articles focused on EarthCoop's distinctive definitions and the relationship among land, rights, freedom, equality, responsibility, participation, and economic design.

Examples of suitable content intents:

- «عدالت در EarthCoop چگونه تعریف می‌شود؟»
- «برابری بنیادین با برابری نتیجه چه تفاوتی دارد؟»
- «آزادی مسئولانه در یک نظام مشارکتی یعنی چه؟»
- «چرا جایگاه زمین و منابع برای نظریه عدالت EarthCoop بنیادی است؟»
- «مالکیت خصوصی بر دسترنج و حق همگانی نسبت به منابع چگونه با هم جمع می‌شوند؟»

These pages should educate first and explain EarthCoop's model second; they should not be written as slogans.

## 5. Economic model

### 5.1 Economy name

The formal name of EarthCoop's economic model is:

**اقتصاد آزاد مردمی**

EarthCoop is designed to realize this model as an economic framework intended to be fair.

### 5.2 Core definition

The model combines:

- freedom to produce, work, create, invest, trade, and own the legitimate fruits of one's work;
- broad participation of people in ownership, investment, production, and economic decision-making;
- protection of common rights in land and foundational resources;
- prevention of monopoly and unjustified concentration of economic power;
- healthy competition and open economic participation;
- transparency, accountability, and auditability;
- security and legitimate privacy;
- responsibility toward shared resources and future generations.

### 5.3 Main economic sub-concepts

#### اقتصاد مردمی

A public-facing concept connecting EarthCoop to broad participation by people in ownership, production, investment, cooperation, and economic decision-making.

#### اقتصاد شیشه‌ای

EarthCoop's intended meaning is **transparent and secure economy**.

The metaphor is:

- «بانک شفاف و چراغ‌ها روشن» — rules, institutional financial activity, public funds, projects, audit trails, and accountable flows should be observable to the legitimate extent;
- «شیشه ضدگلوله و نشکن» — transparency must not make the system fragile; security, integrity, anti-fraud controls, resilience, and legitimate privacy remain essential.

Related public search concepts may include:

- شفافیت اقتصادی
- شفافیت مالی
- بانکداری شفاف
- حسابرسی عمومی
- پاسخ‌گویی مالی
- مبارزه با فساد
- امنیت مالی
- حریم خصوصی مالی
- قابلیت رهگیری تراکنش‌ها
- اعتماد عمومی

«اقتصاد شیشه‌ای» should be developed as an EarthCoop-owned semantic concept while also being connected to these established generic concepts.

## 8. SEO architecture: Entity + Topic Cluster

The selected approach is **Entity + Topic Cluster**, not isolated keyword pages.

The system must model:

`EarthCoop concept/entity -> real user queries -> variants -> search intent -> semantic fit -> stability -> target content type -> canonical target URL -> supporting content -> internal links -> evidence -> Search Console data`

This prevents creating one page per keyword and reduces cannibalization.

`docs.earthcoop.ir` remains the formal/legal/institutional source of truth.

## 10. Content style and trust policy

Articles should educate before promoting EarthCoop.

For a generic query such as «اقتصاد مردمی چیست؟» the content should:

1. explain the generic concept fairly;
2. describe major interpretations or tensions where relevant;
3. distinguish established external concepts from EarthCoop's own model;
4. then explain EarthCoop's specific approach;
5. link to the relevant Pillar and official document.

Three evidence classes must remain distinguishable:

- independent authoritative sources for generic external concepts;
- official EarthCoop documents for EarthCoop rules and definitions;
- explicit EarthCoop analysis for its own proposed model or comparative interpretation.

## 11. Keyword research system

A maintained **SEO Topic Map** should store, for each concept/opportunity:

- `topic/entity`
- `official_earthcoop_term`
- `primary_query`
- `query_variants`
- `intent`
- `earthcoop_fit`
- `stability`
- `target_type` (`pillar`, `blog`, `glossary`, `docs`)
- `target_url`
- `supporting_content`
- `internal_links`
- `evidence`
- `search_console_impressions`
- `search_console_clicks`
- `search_console_ctr`
- `search_console_average_position`
- `status`

### Research workflow

1. Extract terms and concepts from EarthCoop documents and product behavior.
2. Research the language real Persian users use for those concepts.
3. Identify search intent.
4. Cluster semantically related queries.
5. Verify real fit with EarthCoop.
6. Assign one canonical target page per intent.
7. Score and prioritize.
8. Publish only stable, useful content.
9. Use Search Console data to refine titles, sections, links, and future article choices.

### Opportunity scoring

Score at least:

- relevance to EarthCoop;
- search-intent fit;
- EarthCoop authority/distinctiveness potential;
- conceptual stability;
- competition / SERP difficulty;
- useful content depth;
- mission value;
- available evidence;
- observed Search Console demand once data exists.

Search volume, if reliable data becomes available, is an input but not the sole decision-maker.

## 13. Indexing policy

Index only concepts and claims that are sufficiently stable.

Rules:

- stable foundational philosophy: indexable;
- stable governance mechanics: indexable;
- stable economic principles: indexable;
- evolving detailed monetary/economic parameters: do not promote as permanent SEO content until stable;
- experimental product features: no dedicated SEO claims until validated;
- drafts/private/member/admin/search-result pages: remain excluded according to technical SEO policy.

## 14. Legacy blog policy

Existing blog posts are described by the project owner as test/sample content.

Before deletion:

- check whether any URL is indexed;
- check Search Console impressions/clicks;
- check known backlinks/referrals where possible;
- preserve any unexpectedly valuable URL via rewrite or 301 redirect.

If no value exists, the sample posts can be removed and replaced with the new topic-cluster content program.

## 15. Internal linking model

Preferred chain:

`Blog article -> Pillar -> official Docs`

and, where useful:

`Pillar -> selected supporting articles -> official Docs`

Related articles should cross-link when they address distinct intents.

Every major cluster should have a clearly identifiable hub page.

## 16. Multilingual readiness

Concept IDs should be language-neutral internally where feasible.

Example conceptual mapping:

`transparent-economy`

- FA: اقتصاد شیشه‌ای / اقتصاد شفاف و امن

## 17. Technical SEO items already identified

- all public indexable routes vs sitemap membership;

## 18. Measurement

Google Search Console becomes an ongoing research instrument, not merely an indexing console.

Track by cluster and page:

- impressions;
- clicks;
- CTR;
- average position;
- actual query variants;
- page/query mismatch;
- cannibalization;
- indexing state;
- growth of non-brand discovery traffic.

Observed query language should feed back into the SEO Topic Map.

## 19. Guardrails

Do not:

- stuff keywords;
- create one page per synonym;
- create thin doorway pages;
- state that adjacent concepts are equivalent when they are only similar;
- claim external recognition for EarthCoop-specific terms without evidence;
- index unstable details;
- publish large volumes of low-value generated text;
- let blog pages contradict official Docs.

## 20. Roadmap after written-spec approval

### Phase A — research and inventory

- complete repository/public-route SEO audit;
- inventory current blog content;
- extract EarthCoop entities and stable concepts from official docs;
- build the first SEO Topic Map;
- perform Persian SERP/query research for each major cluster;
- prioritize initial Pillars and article clusters.

### Phase B — technical hardening

- close sitemap gaps such as `/privacy`;
- verify public route indexability contract;
- improve structured data/breadcrumb infrastructure where needed;
- prepare stable Pillar routing/content architecture;
- add tests before implementation changes.

### Phase C — first Persian Pillars

Start with a limited set of highest-value stable concepts rather than all proposed URLs at once. Likely early candidates:

- EarthCoop identity;
- economy / اقتصاد آزاد مردمی;
- governance;
- justice/rights;
- cooperative/global or cooperative/digital;
- commons/shared ownership.

Final order depends on keyword research.

### Phase D — blog reset and content clusters

- audit and retire/redirect test posts;
- publish high-quality supporting article clusters;
- establish internal links to Pillars and Docs;
- maintain editorial and source-quality standards.

### Phase E — measurement and iteration

- submit/verify updated sitemap;
- inspect representative URLs;
- monitor query discovery in Search Console;
- expand clusters according to real data;
- detect and fix cannibalization.

### Phase F — English and Arabic

After Persian architecture proves stable:

- independent keyword research for English;
- independent keyword research for Arabic;
- multilingual URL/hreflang rollout;
- culturally/semantically appropriate content adaptation.

## 21. Success criteria

The strategy succeeds when:

- EarthCoop ranks not only for its brand but progressively for relevant non-brand Persian queries;
- each important EarthCoop concept has one clear canonical public explanation;
- broad philosophical concepts such as justice, freedom, equality, and rights are connected to EarthCoop's distinctive model without becoming vague slogan pages;
- the blog becomes a coherent knowledge library rather than a set of sample posts;
- Pillars, blog articles, and official Docs reinforce rather than compete with each other;
- proprietary concepts such as اقتصاد آزاد مردمی, اقتصاد شیشه‌ای, نجم هدا, نجم‌بهار, and بهار gain clear semantic definitions associated with EarthCoop;
- Search Console data continuously improves the topic map and content roadmap;
- future English and Arabic SEO can be added without redesigning the Persian architecture.
