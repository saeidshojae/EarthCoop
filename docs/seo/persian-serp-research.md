# Persian SERP / Query Research — Phase 1

Date: 2026-10-05

Companion data: `docs/seo/persian-keyword-map.csv`

## Method

This research combines:

1. EarthCoop's approved entity map and official project documents (semantic truth / stability gate).
2. Live Persian web/SERP exploration for actual terminology, result framing and neighboring concepts.
3. Intent clustering rather than one-page-per-keyword mapping.

No numeric monthly search volume or paid-tool keyword difficulty data was available in this research environment. The map therefore **does not fabricate volume/difficulty numbers**. `competition_observation` is qualitative, based on the visible composition and density of live results, and must later be supplemented with Search Console impressions and, if desired, an external keyword-volume source.

The `priority` field is the sum of five 1–5 dimensions:

- `earthcoop_fit`
- `authority_potential`
- `stability`
- `intent_value`
- `competition_opportunity`

Maximum = 25. This is an editorial/research priority, not a predicted ranking score.

## Key live-search observations

### Cooperation / cooperative identity

Persian results strongly support established phrases around **تعاون**, **اقتصاد تعاونی**, international cooperative movements and cooperative participation. United Nations Persian material uses cooperative/economic-cooperation framing around sustainable development, resilience and social justice. ILO Persian material uses «نهضت جهانی تعاون» and the international cooperative movement.

The exact phrase **«تعاونی جهانی»** has weaker/less standardized result ownership than broad cooperation/international-cooperative language. That is useful for EarthCoop: it is a plausible discovery phrase, but copy must define the concept rather than pretending it is a universally fixed legal category.

**«تعاونی دیجیتال»** exists in Persian search results, but current results can refer to specific real-estate/investment products. Therefore the phrase is semantically useful yet ambiguous. EarthCoop should define its meaning explicitly and avoid relying on the phrase alone.

**«تعاونی پلتفرمی» / Platform Cooperative** has real Persian explanatory content tied to democratic decision-making, shared platform ownership and member control. This makes it a strong comparison cluster, but not an automatic synonym for EarthCoop.

Representative sources observed:

- United Nations in Iran — material on cooperatives, global cooperation, sustainable development and social justice.
- International Labour Organization Persian — International Day of Cooperatives / global cooperative movement.
- Persian platform-cooperative explainer republishing Platform Cooperativism Consortium concepts.
- Persian search results using «تعاونی دیجیتال» for specific investment models, demonstrating ambiguity.

### People's economy / cooperative economy

**«اقتصاد مردمی»** is well-established Persian vocabulary. Live results repeatedly connect it with cooperation, public participation, small capital, production, employment and fairer distribution of opportunity. This validates the term as a strong generic bridge into EarthCoop, while also confirming it is much broader than EarthCoop's proprietary model.

Consequences:

- `/economy/` should own **اقتصاد آزاد مردمی** as EarthCoop's named model.
- A narrower explainer/pillar may own **اقتصاد مردمی** as the generic concept and explain how it fits inside EarthCoop's model.
- **اقتصاد تعاونی** is a supporting/comparison cluster, not a synonym for اقتصاد آزاد مردمی.

Representative result patterns:

- «تعاون؛ مسیر تحقق اقتصاد مردمی…»
- «تعاون رکن مستقل اقتصاد مردمی…»
- academic/public-policy material tying cooperative economics to participation, human/social capital and distribution.

### Glass Economy / transparency / security

The proprietary phrase **«اقتصاد شیشه‌ای»** does not have a stable public meaning comparable to EarthCoop's approved definition. That creates an entity-building opportunity.

Generic Persian search language is much stronger around:

- شفافیت اقتصادی
- شفافیت مالی
- شفافیت بانک‌ها / بانکداری شفاف
- پاسخ‌گویی
- حسابرسی
- مبارزه با فساد

Live results commonly frame economic/financial transparency as visibility of rules, budgets, decisions, information and accountable flows. IMF transparency material adds an important generic pattern: transparency is linked to accountability and effectiveness, but is not itself a complete governance model.

EarthCoop's distinctive opportunity is therefore **not merely transparency**. It is the combined query/problem space of:

- شفافیت + امنیت
- شفافیت + حریم خصوصی
- حسابرسی + عدم افشای غیرضروری داده خصوصی

That maps directly to the approved «بانک شفاف و چراغ‌ها روشن / شیشه ضدگلوله و نشکن» definition and to EarthCoop's financial rules requiring auditability plus privacy/security.

### Governance / participation

**«حکمرانی مشارکتی»** is established Persian academic/public-policy terminology. Visible results repeatedly associate it with:

- citizen/stakeholder participation;
- decision-making;
- oversight;
- transparency;
- accountability;
- trust;
- participatory budgeting/projects;
- technology-supported participation.

This is a strong semantic fit for EarthCoop and should be a primary generic query for the governance pillar.

**«حاکمیت مشارکتی»** also appears, but current Persian result language more consistently uses «حکمرانی مشارکتی». The map therefore treats the former as an adjacent wording variant, not a separate target URL.

### Elections / liquid democracy

EarthCoop's formal term **«انتخابات دائمی بدون نامزد»** comes from its own documents and is distinctive. Persian SERP authority around this exact phrase appears sparse, which means the opportunity is high but demand cannot be inferred from result scarcity.

The generic/neighbor search space includes:

- انتخابات مستمر
- انتخابات پویا
- رأی قابل تغییر
- پس گرفتن رأی
- روش‌های نوین انتخاباتی
- دموکراسی سیال

Persian material exists for **دموکراسی سیال (Liquid Democracy)** and frames it as an attempt to combine/flex between direct and representative democracy. This supports a comparison article. It does **not** prove EarthCoop's permanent candidate-less election model is liquid democracy.

Decision: one primary election pillar should own the continuous/permanent candidate-less EarthCoop mechanic; liquid democracy remains a comparison article/section.

### Local governance / self-governance

Persian academic results use:

- حکمرانی محلی
- خودگردانی محلی
- تمرکززدایی

and connect them with direct local participation and delegated local authority. The term **خودگردانی محلی** can carry political/legal meanings beyond EarthCoop, so EarthCoop content must define its bounded meaning: local administration of nearby affairs within common foundational rights/standards and a connected local-to-global structure.

This is a strong pillar cluster, but **فدرالیسم** should not be used as an EarthCoop synonym without a separate formal comparison.

### Digital governance / civic tech

Persian results show established use of:

- حکمرانی دیجیتال
- حکمرانی هوشمند
- مشارکت شهروندی
- داده‌محوری
- شفافیت
- نظارت دیجیتال
- هوش مصنوعی in governance

**حکمرانی دیجیتال** is the cleaner exact generic bridge for EarthCoop's formal DG domain. **حکمرانی هوشمند** is adjacent and often tied to smart-city/public-administration contexts.

Persian competition for the literal term **فناوری مدنی** appears lighter than for digital governance. It remains adjacent rather than exact, but may be a useful authority-building explainer around participation technology.

### Money / alternative monetary systems

Persian academic material uses the paired phrase **«پول‌های محلی و مکمل»** and discusses examples such as WIR and local currencies. This confirms a real Persian comparison vocabulary for alternative monetary arrangements.

However:

- `بهار` is officially an internal EarthCoop monetary unit.
- The research does not establish that Bahar meets every definition of complementary currency, community currency, cryptocurrency, stablecoin, or fiat currency.

Therefore all those external classes remain comparison-only until criteria are explicitly checked.

For entity SEO, branded queries such as **«پول بهار»** and **«بهار ارثکوپ»** are better than trying to rank the ambiguous single word «بهار».

### Project finance / participatory investment

Persian SERPs have substantial commercial/regulatory content around:

- تأمین مالی جمعی
- سرمایه‌گذاری جمعی
- سرمایه‌های خرد
- تأمین مالی پروژه

Current Persian usage of **تأمین مالی جمعی** has a relatively specific meaning and, in some jurisdictions such as Iran, a regulated platform context. EarthCoop must therefore not label its own project mechanisms «crowdfunding/تأمین مالی جمعی» unless the exact mechanism and legal context match.

**سرمایه‌گذاری مردمی** is a broader and safer generic bridge for the EarthCoop pillar; crowdfunding is better handled as comparison/explainer content.

### Justice / rights / dignity / freedom / equality

Broad head terms such as **عدالت**, **آزادی**, **برابری** and **حق** are semantically huge and highly competitive. EarthCoop should not create generic thin pages for them.

Live Persian research confirms meaningful established language around:

- عدالت اقتصادی
- حقوق طبیعی
- حق طبیعی
- حقوق ذاتی انسان
- کرامت انسانی / کرامت ذاتی
- حقوق بنیادین

Academic/legal results also show that «حقوق طبیعی» itself has multiple conceptual traditions and can be debated in relation to natural right, human rights, morality and legal theory. This reinforces the approved safety rule: EarthCoop should compare its own foundational-right framework to natural-rights traditions, not claim automatic identity with one school.

EarthCoop has better authority opportunity on relational/long-tail concepts such as:

- عدالت و مالکیت
- مالکیت خصوصی و منابع مشترک
- آزادی مسئولانه
- برابری بنیادین
- حق مشارکت و حق نظارت
- جایگاه زمین و منابع در نظریه عدالت EarthCoop

These are more distinctive and more faithful to EarthCoop's documents than a generic «عدالت چیست؟» page.

## Cannibalization decisions from this research pass

The following variants should share a primary target unless future Search Console data proves materially different intent:

- `تعاونی جهانی` + `تعاون جهانی` → `/cooperative/global/`
- `حکمرانی مشارکتی` + `حاکمیت مشارکتی` + `حکمرانی دموکراتیک` → `/governance/` with narrower supporting articles
- `انتخابات دائمی` + `انتخابات مستمر` + `انتخابات پویا` → `/governance/elections/`, while exact sub-intents such as candidate-less elections can be sections/articles
- `اقتصاد شفاف` + `شفافیت اقتصادی` → `/economy/glass/`, while financial transparency/security/privacy become supporting articles
- `خودگردانی محلی` + `حکمرانی محلی` → `/governance/local-to-global/`
- `پول بهار` + `بهار ارثکوپ` → one eventual Bahar target, if/when the stability gate permits publication

## Important query ambiguities

- **تعاونی دیجیتال:** currently also used as a product/investment label; definition must be explicit.
- **پول مکمل:** can be polluted by non-monetary «مکمل» meanings; use contextual terms and comparison content.
- **پول محلی:** can mean a national/local currency rather than a community-currency model.
- **اقتصاد مشارکتی:** can overlap with the sharing economy; do not assume it means participatory ownership.
- **خودگردانی محلی:** can carry state/political-autonomy meanings beyond EarthCoop's bounded community-governance model.
- **بهار:** highly ambiguous as a common Persian word; branded modifiers are mandatory for SEO targeting.

## Research limitations / evidence labels

The CSV `source_of_query` field distinguishes:

- `project` — terminology comes primarily from EarthCoop source material/approved model decisions.
- `SERP` — wording/competition observation is visible in live Persian web search.
- `project+SERP` — both the internal concept and external wording are supported.
- `inference` — plausible natural-language variant not supported by reliable demand data; must be validated later.

`SERP` means only that the wording/result pattern was observed. It does **not** mean Google search volume is known.

No volume, CPC, paid keyword difficulty, or Google Trends values were available here; none were invented.

High-value reference families encountered in this pass include:

- United Nations Persian cooperative material — international cooperative movement, sustainable development, social justice.

These sources inform terminology/intent. EarthCoop-specific claims must still cite EarthCoop's own formal documents.
